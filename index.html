LinguaLink360 is a modern healthcare technology platform that helps allied healthcare professionals manage patients, clinical documentation, teletherapy, assessments and progress tracking in one place.

Speech therapists, occupational therapists, physiotherapists, audiologists, behavioral therapists and other allied healthcare professionals.

AI drafts SOAP notes, therapy plans and diagnostic suggestions. The clinician always reviews, edits and makes the final decision.

You can configure appointments, patient records, assessments, therapy resources and practice settings within minutes.

Starter gives a doctor account with appointments and visits, 1 resource license and 1 teletherapy license. Professional gives a full hospital account with 3 resource licenses and 3 teletherapy licenses.
