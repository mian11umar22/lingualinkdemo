The platform

What we actually do

From patient management and clinical documentation to teletherapy, assessments and progress tracking, we provide the digital tools clinicians need to streamline operations and improve patient outcomes.

Our goal is simple: let you focus on what matters most, helping individuals achieve their communication, rehabilitation and developmental goals.

Patient management and records

Profiles, history and appointments in one organized place.

Clinical documentation and assessments

Structured notes and assessments that stay with the patient record.

Teletherapy and progress tracking

Deliver sessions remotely and follow progress over time.

AI-assisted notes, plans and diagnostic suggestions

Drafts and suggestions for the clinician to review and approve.

Our principles

What we believe

Simple by design

Clinicians should spend their time with patients, not fighting software. Every screen is made to be quick and clear.

AI that assists

AI handles the repetitive writing. The clinician always reviews, edits and makes the final call.

One secure place

Records, assessments, sessions and progress live together, so nothing gets lost between tools.
